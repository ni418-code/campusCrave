# 🍔 Campus Crave

Campus Crave is a student-focused food ordering platform designed to make ordering food around campus simple, fast, and convenient.

Students can browse available restaurants, explore their menus, select food items, and place orders directly through the platform. Orders are sent directly to the respective restaurant's admin dashboard, eliminating the need for a middleman.

## ✨ Features
🎓 Separate login interfaces for Students and Restaurant Admins
🍽️ Browse available restaurants
📋 View restaurant-specific menus
🛒 Add food items to a cart
📱 Student details including name and phone number
🏪 Restaurant admins can directly view incoming orders
🔄 Real-time order status updates
⏰ Time-based ordering sessions
📍 Fixed food pickup location near the college gate
⏰ Ordering Sessions

### Campus Crave accepts orders only during the following time slots:

☀️ Afternoon Session: 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM
🌙 Night Session: 5:40 PM – 7:30 PM

Orders cannot be placed outside these time periods.

## 🎯 Goal

The goal of Campus Crave is to create a simple and affordable food ordering experience for college students while allowing restaurants to manage their orders directly through a dedicated admin dashboard.
